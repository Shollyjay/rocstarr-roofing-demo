§2.1.10 FAQ — five questions a homeowner actually asks a roofer. The insurance answer deliberately promises nothing an insurer might not honour.

Questions

Before you call

Will my insurance cover a new roof?
Often, if the damage came from a storm. We inspect, photograph everything and hand you the documentation for your claim. We do not file the claim for you, and we cannot promise what an insurer will decide.
Should I repair or replace?
It depends on the age of the roof and how far the damage spreads. A roof in its last few years is usually cheaper to replace than to keep patching. We tell you either way after the inspection.
How long does a replacement take?
Most homes are a one- or two-day job once the materials arrive.
Is the inspection really free?
Yes, and there is no obligation afterwards.
What warranty do I get?
A workmanship warranty from us in writing, plus the manufacturer's warranty on the materials.
